# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Buyers evaluating a physical-security and contracting vendor in Saudi Arabia. Four overlapping groups:

- **Business & bank buyers** - procurement officers, branch operations and facility managers sourcing safes, vault/cashier-room fit-out, CCTV, access control, and maintenance contracts.
- **Contractors & integrators** - construction firms and security integrators sourcing hardware plus installation for their own projects.
- **Government & institutions** - public-sector and institutional buyers with security and compliance requirements.
- **Individual consumers** - homeowners buying personal safes and smart locks.

Context of use: they arrive to judge whether this company is credible and capable, to find the right product or service, and to request a quote or make contact. They are mostly non-technical (procurement and facilities staff, some older users) and decide on credibility fast. Arabic speakers first; English speakers second.

## Product Purpose

The company website for **شركة أحلام الجزيرة للمقاولات والصيانة / Aljazeera Dreams Co.** - a Saudi security-solutions and contracting company.

Offering: security safes and vault doors, bank and cashier-room fit-out, CCTV and access-control systems, smart locks, heavy-safe transport and installation, and periodic maintenance / operating contracts. The site also carries a product catalog and brand pages for the hardware lines it supplies.

Fully bilingual: Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR).

Success looks like: qualified quote requests and direct contact from business, contractor, and government buyers; catalog browsing that ends in an inquiry rather than a bounce; a first impression that reads as an established specialist, not a template.

## Brand Personality

Modern and clean; established and trusted. Three words: **secure, precise, established.**

Voice: confident and straightforward. States capability plainly, backs it with real projects and specifications, and never resorts to discounts, urgency, or hype. The tone of a specialist that banks and government bodies already rely on.

Emotional goals: security, confidence, competence. A visitor should feel they are dealing with people who can be trusted with a vault.

## Anti-references

- **The current site's generic Elementor / WordPress template look** - stock content blocks, clip-art icons, default spacing, a template hero. This is the primary thing to move away from.
- **Flashy consumer e-commerce** - discount badges, countdown timers, loud sale banners, dense product grids.
- **Neon tech-startup / crypto aesthetics** - dark backgrounds, glowing gradients, sci-fi styling. Wrong industry signal.
- **Cheap locksmith / hardware-store look** - busy, low-budget, dated clip-art, careless typography.

## Design Principles

1. **The brand colors are locked.** The existing palette is a client (owner) requirement and must be carried through the redesign unchanged. See Constraints below and DESIGN.md for exact values.
2. **Arabic is the source of truth.** Every layout and copy decision is made in Arabic RTL first; the English site is a faithful mirror with equal care, not an afterthought.
3. **Show capability, don't claim it.** Real installations, real specs, real brand names over stock photography and adjectives.
4. **Weight signals security.** Solid structure, deliberate spacing, clear alignment. Nothing flimsy, cramped, or decorative-for-its-own-sake.
5. **Every page has one clear next step.** Route the visitor toward a quote request or contact without hard-sell tactics.

## Constraints

- **Color palette is fixed by the owner and may not be changed.** The redesign must reuse the existing brand colors exactly. Canonical values live in DESIGN.md; the primary is deep teal `#047E79` with turquoise accent `#1FBAB3`. New shades may only be tints/shades derived from these for states and surfaces - no new hues.
- Bilingual Arabic (RTL, primary) + English (LTR). Full structural and visual parity between the two.
- Existing content, product catalog, and URL structure from the static clone should be preserved where practical.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: text contrast, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states. Matters for government and institutional buyers.
- **RTL-first** with complete bilingual parity; validate every layout in both directions.
- Readable for non-technical procurement and facilities staff and for older users: generous type sizing, clear hierarchy, plain language, no icon-only or jargon-only labels.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
